# 📊 Online Company Sales Dashboard

An end-to-end sales analysis project for an online company, built using **SQL** and **Power BI**, covering global vs. UK performance, sales by country, product, and month.

---

## 🛠️ Tools Used
- **SQL** for data extraction and initial querying
- **Power Query** for cleaning and reducing the dataset size
- **Power BI** for data modeling and building the final dashboard

---

## 🔄 Workflow
1. **Extraction:** Wrote SQL queries to pull sales data from the database.
2. **Cleaning & Preparation:** Removed nulls and duplicates, and reduced the dataset size to make it easier to process.
3. **Modeling & Visualization:** Organized and connected the data in Power BI, then built the interactive dashboard.

---

## 📈 Dashboard Pages

### 1️⃣ Online Company Sales — Overview
- Total orders: **536K**
- Total sales: **30.30T**
- Total quantity sold: **6M**
- Comparison between **global** performance and **UK-only** performance (485K orders / 30.24T sales / 5M quantity)
- Top customers by sales, quantity, and number of orders

### 2️⃣ Sales per Month
- Trends in sales, orders, and quantities sold across 12 months to identify peak and low seasons

### 3️⃣ Sales Outside UK
- Total sales outside the UK: **59.38bn**
- Total orders outside the UK: **51K**
- Top-performing countries: **Germany, EIRE (Ireland), France, Netherlands, Australia**

### 4️⃣ Sold Products
- Product performance by sales, order count, and quantity sold
- Top products include: Cream Hanging Heart T-Light Holder, Popcorn Holder, Regency Cakestand 3 Tier, Jumbo Bag Red Retrospot

---

## 🎯 Project Goal
Build a clear picture of sales performance to support decisions on:
- The most profitable markets geographically
- Best-performing products
- Highest-value customer segments
- Seasonal sales trends
